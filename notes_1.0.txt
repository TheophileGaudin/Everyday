New voice control agent

-> Create a Gemini API key in Google AI Studio, copy it on your phone, then paste it in the glasses' Gemini settings.
-> Uses the Gemini API free tier: no charges as long as billing stays disabled on the key's project. If you hit the free limits, the agent stops with an error until they reset.
-> Push-to-talk (phone button or glasses): it doesn't talk back, it acts and shows short text answers if needed.
-> Can take picture/movies/analyze what you see, search the web, show/hide widgets, change what they display, use the browser, and start or stop walking navigation on the map.
-> Works on the glasses' Wi-Fi or through your phone's internet (4G or Wi-Fi), without the RayNeo app.
-> Automatic model choice: the newest Flash-Lite model, efficient and generous on the free tier. You can select a stronger model manually for complex requests; Flash-Lite handles almost all everyday requests.
